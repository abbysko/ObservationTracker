# Vision

## Purpose

ObservationTracker is a personal nature observation journal that helps outdoor enthusiasts, citizen scientists, and nature lovers record, organize, and reflect on their encounters with the natural world.

## Problem Statement

Nature observers currently rely on paper notebooks, generic note-taking apps, or complex citizen-science platforms that are either too simple (losing structure) or too complex (requiring species expertise to submit). There is no lightweight, offline-friendly tool that:

- Works in the browser without an account
- Structures observations consistently
- Lets users revisit and filter past sightings
- Provides simple personal statistics and maps

## Target Users

- Casual birdwatchers and wildlife spotters
- Hikers who want to record what they see
- Gardeners tracking seasonal plant activity
- Students doing field study
- Citizen scientists contributing informal data

## Core Value Propositions

1. **Frictionless logging** — log an observation in under 30 seconds
2. **Offline-first** — works without internet, stores data locally
3. **No account required** — privacy-respecting, zero sign-up barrier
4. **Cross-platform** — web first, iOS later
5. **Personal insights** — trends, maps, and statistics on your own data

## Long-Term Vision

Eventually extend to optional cloud sync, sharing observations with communities, and integration with existing databases (eBird, iNaturalist) for species identification assistance.
